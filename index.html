Privacy Policy for Sparrow

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Sparrow does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Data we collect

None. Sparrow has no network functionality and contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking software of any kind. No information about you or your device is sent to us or to any third party, because the app does not communicate over the internet at all.

Data stored on your device

Sparrow saves your game history and statistics locally on your device so it can show your records across sessions. This information never leaves your device. It is not backed up to our servers, because there are no servers. Deleting the app permanently removes this data.

Third parties

Sparrow includes no third-party SDKs and shares no data with anyone.

Children

Sparrow does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Sparrow changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
